# Proposed experience

A food business and a receiving partner publish compatible windows. The platform proposes a feasible run to a volunteer, shows why it fits, and lets the partner confirm the quantity it actually received. The partner then issues an availability update through a recipient's preferred channel. The primary interface is a volunteer pickup and handoff, supported by short donor and partner flows.

## Initial scope

- One Colombo catchment, a small set of verified food businesses and one staffed receiving partner in the prototype scenario. Start with partner-approved packaged surplus with intact labelling as a demonstrable category. Expand to other categories only after the partner and local food safety authority confirm applicable procedures, infrastructure and responsibilities.
- Donor enters category, quantity, label or allergen information as applicable, handling status and availability. Missing or unsuitable offers move to review or decline.
- Receiving coordinator sets a window, capacity and acceptable categories. The system reserves capacity for an accepted assignment and releases it on cancellation or expiry.
- Volunteer sees the proposed run and confirms, declines, or cancels. A coordinator may assign another feasible volunteer. If a configured eligibility condition would be missed, the attempt closes without a handoff.
- The donor and volunteer confirm pickup. On arrival the partner logs the actual accepted quantity or rejects the batch and escalates under its procedure. Only the final accepted outcome enables a recipient availability update. The update gives the site, time, food and relevant ingredient information and a way to ask privately about suitability. A changed quantity or withdrawn batch can trigger an update through the same channel.

## Accessibility, privacy and safety

Prototype compact pages with text labels, high contrast, accessible touch targets, keyboard focus, error text and a clear language choice. Keep maps optional. Offer a text summary when a connection is weak, distinguish locally saved input from confirmed status, and define a partner call or SMS fallback. Test Sinhala and Tamil copy with fluent speakers and preserve names, times and quantities through translation.

Use approximate zones in browse and matching views and reveal operational addresses to assigned roles. Keep recipient participation private and optional. Volunteer dashboards receive aggregate outcomes. Handle changing, damaged or unsuitable batches through the partner's procedure and log the exception without rewarding completion. See [local evidence and constraints](../02-research/sri-lanka-evidence.md).

## What a Round 2 demo should prove

Show the team completing a handoff, handling a late cancellation and making an informed recipient choice. Stage the demo with fictional records and clearly marked simulated recommendations. Primary measures for a later real pilot: confirmed handoffs within the agreed window divided by eligible offers, delivered portions confirmed by the partner, cancellation reasons, and recipient-reported usefulness of updates. Treat confirmed portions as portions received by the partner. Use separate evidence before claiming meals distributed, unique households reached or waste prevented.
