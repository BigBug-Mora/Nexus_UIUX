# Round 1 wireframe prompts

Use these prompts to create original pencil or low-fidelity digital sketches with the registered team. Use fictional data. The final PDF should contain actual rough sketches with short captions connecting the interaction to its evidence or assumption.

| Panel | Must show | Decision or state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A · Donor offer | Quantity, item and label details, pickup availability, eligibility check and submit | Show a missing-field state that holds an offer. |
| B · Receiving partner capacity | Receiving hours, acceptable categories, available capacity and pause action | Indicate whether the partner can accept this batch. |
| C · Volunteer recommendation | Pickup window, estimated route time, handoff location or general area, why it fits, accept and decline | Show an explainable match and what changes if the slot disappears. |
| D · Handoff and exception | Donor pickup confirmation, partner accepted quantity, report mismatch or cancel | Contrast visually distinct status labels for assigned, picked up, accepted, reassigned and closed. |
| E · Recipient update | Confirmed site and time, food and ingredient information where available, preferred channel and private help | Include a changed or withdrawn update. Sketch a basic SMS or coordinator-assisted version. |
| F · Progress and impact | Optional team mission, verified private credit and aggregate portions | Show where points and badge appear and preview the opt-in team leaderboard. |

Draw one solid path from A and B to C, then D and E, and a branch from D for cancellation. Use F after confirmation. For a compact proposal show A–D together and E–F together, with three annotations on why the match appears, why recipients are notified at that point and which event earns credit.

Walk through the draft sketches with a donor, a volunteer and an adult recipient where access permits. Ask them to say who acts next, whether availability is confirmed, and how to stop or decline. Record confusion and revise the sketches before export.
