# Sri Lanka desk research

Use the evidence IDs when drafting. Preserve each source's geography, date, sample and unit. The design implications are interpretations to test with local users.

| ID | What the source supports | Design implication and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | A 2021 paper based on Colombo stakeholder interviews records donor concerns about hygiene, reputation and liability, and reports a short window between evening release and receiving demand, including a nursing-home example. Transport and temperature-control capacity also surfaced as constraints. [S1 §3.2.5](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/2/838) | Investigate explicit receiving hours, who can approve an offer, and who handles exceptions. The findings are historical and mainly stakeholder accounts. They establish a plausible local problem, with its current prevalence and local workflow still open. |
| E2 | In one Colombo hotel audit, 21% of prepared food was wasted in the initial assessment. Buffet surplus was 46% of the measured waste, customer plate leftovers 33%, and preparation waste 21%. The initial phase took place between COVID-related lockdowns. [S2, Findings](https://archive.iwmi.org/wle/thrive/2021/11/12/what-cost-food-we-waste-and-how-can-we-change-behaviours-address-growing-challenge/) | Record the type and handling of surplus separately. This is a site-specific prevention case. Waste volume and buffet surplus cannot be translated into safe donation volume. |
| E3 | WFP reports that 24% of households were moderately food insecure in its August–September 2023 survey. WFP later ran remote monitoring after Cyclone Ditwah, including a January 2026 survey. [S3, historical survey](https://www.wfp.org/publications/household-food-security-surveys-sri-lanka), [S4, post-cyclone series](https://www.wfp.org/publications/sri-lanka-remote-household-food-security-surveys) | Establishes broader need and how context can change. Neither source establishes availability, food preferences or demand at the proposed receiving site. Use the dated 2023 figure only as historical context. |
| E4 | The 2024 census reports digital literacy among people aged 5+ at 67.6% nationally, 76.8% urban, 66.4% rural, 50.2% estate rural, and 78.7% in Colombo district. [S5 Tables 7.12–7.13](https://www.statistics.gov.lk/Resource/en/Population/CPH_2024/CPH2024_Final_Eng.pdf) | Test low-data and partner-assisted access. Digital literacy figures describe a population measure and leave household device sharing, data budgets and language preferences open. |
| E5 | The Colombo study reports that, in mid-2019, Robin Hood Army volunteers used WhatsApp to coordinate evening pickups, mainly from bakeries. The study also records volunteer time and refrigerated transport and storage constraints across two local initiatives. A 2022 Uber Eats announcement documents a separate dry-ration campaign with the Sri Lankan chapter across Colombo. [S1 Box 2](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/2/838), [S6](https://www.uber.com/lk/en/newsroom/uber-eats-partners-with-rha/) | Shows local precedent for organizing volunteers and businesses. The campaign is evidence of dry-ration coordination. Present-day capacity, pickup availability and handling of prepared meals need direct verification. |
| E6 | Sri Lanka's Food (Hygiene) Regulations include food processing, transport, distribution, handling and storage in their scope for establishments. [S7 reg. 2](https://eohfs.health.gov.lk/food/images/pdf/regulations/food_hygiene_regulations_2011_en.pdf) | Before any real trial, ask the receiving partner and the relevant Public Health Inspector or Medical Officer of Health to confirm applicable handling procedures, food categories and responsibilities. Use synthetic listings for competition demonstrations. |
| E7 | ACT Foundation describes operating community kitchens and a school meal program in Sri Lanka. [S8](https://www.actfoundation.lk/act4hunger/) | Shows a community-mediated model worth learning from. Acceptance of externally prepared surplus and participation in this project are unconfirmed. |
| E8 | Sri Lanka's Data Protection Authority publishes the PDPA and its 2025 amendment. [S9](https://dpa.gov.lk/guidelines) | Prototype using fictional contact information and limited location detail. Verify current legal obligations and obtain partner review before a real pilot. |
| E9 | Social listening on r/srilanka reveals that Colombo bakeries discard surplus shorteats past 8:30 PM due to liability fears and food poisoning risks, while community donors face rejected drop-offs at shelters due to mismatched dinner schedules (6:30–7:00 PM) and lack of live capacity indicators. [Appendix A3–A5](../round-1-proposal.md#appendix-a--source-screenshots-and-community-context) | Design for explicit food safety screening, pre-matched evening windows before closing, and time-stamped status to eliminate wasted trips and legal hesitation. |

**Key local gap:** The published interviews support investigating a time-sensitive handoff. The team still needs evidence from present-day volunteers and adult recipients about channel preferences, missed pickups, travel, food suitability and what happens after a receiving site accepts a batch.
