# Fieldwork plan

## If the submission window is still open today

1. Confirm the portal and cutoff. Divide the work into interviews, synthesis, sketches and PDF assembly across whoever is available.
2. Try to reach one adult from each required group for a 10–15 minute conversation. A receiving coordinator can add operational evidence. Seek an adult recipient's own perspective separately and keep any proxy testimony labelled.
3. Reconstruct a recent real handoff or missed attempt using the [guide](interview-guide.md). Where field access falls short, use the desk research, mark assumptions by group, describe recruitment limits and keep a clear validation plan.
4. Spend the remaining time checking the evidence-to-design links and sketching one success and one failure path.

## If more research time is available

- Recruit **2–3 food businesses**, **2–3 volunteers** including a newer or infrequent volunteer, **2–3 adult recipients**, and **one receiving coordinator**. These targets are practical qualitative goals. Report the actual sample and how participants were reached.
- Start with an accessible Colombo or Greater Colombo catchment. Recruit businesses with an identifiable closing shift, ask a volunteer organization about actual routes, and ask a community organization to forward an opt-in invitation. Speak in participants' preferred language, using a trusted interpreter with consent where needed.
- Observe one partner-approved handoff or watch staff reenact a recent one with fictional details. Record the sequence, the channel, who agreed to what, where uncertainty started, and which event ended the handoff. A short survey can follow if interviews uncover a specific question to check across more people.
- Discuss categories and exception handling with the partner and local food safety authority before planning any real food movement. Use paper scenarios to test the concept in this phase.

## Synthesis

Enter evidence notes for each event, label direct observation, participant account, desk research and inference, and cluster recurring causes. For each cause, record a counterexample, who is affected, an implication and an open question. Prioritize failures that block a safe handoff or undermine access and dignity. Change the scope if participants point to a different repeated bottleneck. Compare the final concept to the existing workflow using identical scenarios.

## Research participation

Make participation opt-in, voluntary and independent of food access. Use participant codes. Seek separate permission for recordings and attributed quotes. Collect anonymous notes where possible, keep raw records in a team-approved restricted location, and keep contact details, recordings, addresses and identifiable photos out of Git and the proposal. Decide who may access notes and when to delete them before starting. Ask about channels and preferences without requesting proof of need.
