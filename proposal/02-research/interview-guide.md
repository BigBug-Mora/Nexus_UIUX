# Interview and observation guide

## Opening

“We’re students researching how surplus food is offered, collected and received. This is voluntary, you can skip anything, and taking part has no effect on services or support. We’ll use anonymized notes for a competition proposal. Is it okay to continue and take notes?” Ask separately for recording or a direct quotation.

## Ask about the last real occasion first

| Participant | Prompts |
| --- | --- |
| Donor | Walk through the last time food was left near closing. When was the amount known? Who could approve sharing it? How was it packed and held? How were ingredient details communicated? What changed when pickup was late or missing? |
| Volunteer | Tell us about your last successful and last difficult run. How did you hear about it and decide whether it fit? What did you need to know about route, timing or carrying capacity? What happened if plans changed? What makes you return or drop out? |
| Adult recipient | When food or information about it was available, how did you hear? Which times and locations were workable? What helped you decide whether the food suited your household? How would you prefer to hear about a change privately? What language, device or access limits should we consider? |
| Receiving partner | Describe how you decide whether to accept a donation today. What quantities, timing and dietary information are manageable? What happens if a driver cancels, a batch is unsuitable, or more people arrive than expected? How do you currently communicate availability? |

Ask each group what their normal device and data access are, who shares the phone, and which language or channel they use when something is urgent. Ask “What happened next?” before “Would you use this?” Capture timings only if the participant recalls them.
