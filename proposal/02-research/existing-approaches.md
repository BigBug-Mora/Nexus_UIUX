# Existing approaches

These comparisons describe published workflows. Product pages are evidence of what a provider describes publicly. They leave adoption, reliability and current operations open.

| Approach | Public evidence | What to compare in a local walkthrough |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Robin Hood Army, Colombo | Interviews document WhatsApp-coordinated evening pickups in 2019 and a partner announcement describes dry-ration collection. [S1 §3.2.5 and Box 2](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/2/838), [S6](https://www.uber.com/lk/en/newsroom/uber-eats-partners-with-rha/) | How a driver is assigned and who has authority to accept, reassign or decline a batch |
| ACT Foundation community kitchens | Describes neighborhood kitchens providing meals and welcoming community participation. [S8](https://www.actfoundation.lk/act4hunger/) | How people receive information and how kitchens manage timing, quantity and dietary needs |
| MealBridge | Sri Lankan site presents listings, claims, timing and volunteer runs. Only public pages were reviewed. [S10](https://mealbridge.lk/), [Help](https://mealbridge.lk/help) | Compare the designed experience for confirmed receiving capacity, reasons for a match, volunteer cancellation and a private recipient update. Treat the site's displayed listing and volunteer counts as unverified. |
| Food Rescue Hero | Describes volunteer alerts, guided pickups and deliveries, and dispatch tools. [S11](https://foodrescuehero.org/our-product/) | Pickup dispatch and volunteer notifications have precedent. Compare how existing operations handle release times, delivery risks and exceptions. |
| Informal calls and messages | A local baseline to observe during fieldwork. The historical Robin Hood Army example establishes WhatsApp as a precedent. Current channel use and its limitations need verification. | Reconstruct one past coordination attempt, including what got resolved quickly and what was difficult with the usual channel. |

Proposed differentiation to validate: a Colombo handoff with a receiving partner declaring its capacity and hours, a volunteer seeing why a run fits, and recipients getting an update privately through the partner only after arrival is confirmed. Bring this as a workflow hypothesis to interviews. Revisit it if existing practices already handle the same failure well or if participants value a different improvement.
