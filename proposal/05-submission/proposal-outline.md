# Proposal assembly guide

A suggested seven-page narrative within the brief's proposed 6–8 body pages. Fit a small title block and team details on page 1. Use separate references pages.

| Page | Content to assemble | Working material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | In one paragraph, define the site, three groups, chosen failure and stakes. Cite one Colombo finding. | [Problem](../03-synthesis/problem-and-assumptions.md) |
| 2 | Summarize research method, actual sample and three group findings. Pair each pain point with a cause, evidence ID and limitation. | [Evidence](../02-research/sri-lanka-evidence.md), [fieldwork](../02-research/fieldwork-plan.md) |
| 3 | Three personas with goals, frustrations, context, devices and data access. Mark assumptions or evidence. | [Personas](../03-synthesis/personas-and-journey.md) |
