# Persona and journey working notes

These are provisional role hypotheses for the team to revise after research. Every behavior, emotion and access constraint below needs validation. Role names are descriptive and the examples are fictional.

| Persona | Context and goal | Frustrations to test | Device, data and language to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Donor · closing-shift lead | A food business has a small batch eligible for screening at closing. The lead wants to register it quickly and see whether a pickup is confirmed. | Approvals, missing labels, repeated calls and uncertainty about who is arriving. | Shared staff phone during shift, short form using repeat donor details, preferred Sinhala/Tamil/English. |
| Volunteer · occasional evening runner | Has some time after work or class and chooses nearby routes. Wants to finish a run within an available time and with suitable transport. | Unexpected travel, inaccurate directions, unclear carrying needs and reluctance to cancel late. | Mobile-first use, intermittent data, text instructions and a call option. |
| Recipient · adult using a community meal service | Plans around household commitments and wants to know when suitable food is actually available nearby. | Wasted travel, unclear dietary or ingredient details, public identification and unreliable updates. | Could share a handset or use a basic phone. Verify preferred language and whether phone, SMS or in-person information fits. |

The receiving coordinator is a supporting operational role. Use their evidence to understand capacity and handoff decisions. Add direct recipient evidence for the recipient persona.

## Lead journey hypothesis · volunteer evening pickup

| Stage | Action and touchpoint | Possible feeling | Current friction to check | Proposed support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Need | Has an available evening slot and wants to help. | Interested, time-conscious | Sends messages to find out what is needed. | Set an availability window and travel area. |
| Evaluate | Opens a run and checks pickup, destination and load. | Unsure | Unknown receiving hours, quantity and travel time. | Show a short explanation with the destination's acceptance and the time estimate. |
| Commit | Accepts or declines a pickup. | Cautious | No clear owner or cancellation route. | Reserve the run on acceptance and show a cancellation action. |
| Collect | Meets donor and checks package details. | Focused | Food or handling information changes at pickup. | Confirm quantity and handling details or stop and report a mismatch. |
| Hand over | Gets the partner's acceptance or rejection. | Relieved or concerned | Unclear if the partner is ready or who handles rejection. | Two-role confirmation and explicit exception states. |
| Close | Receives outcome and optional progress. Partner informs recipients privately. | Satisfied or disappointed | No reliable feedback or premature alerts. | Show verified outcome and use the partner's agreed recipient channel. |

Before exporting, have the registered team redraw its own journey from actual accounts. Mark each feeling as reported or hypothesized, and show at least one failure where the flow ends safely.
